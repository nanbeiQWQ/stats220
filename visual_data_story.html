The 8am Struggle: Weather, Class Type, and Student Turnout

Who turns up at 8 am?

Differences in average attendance by course type

Lectures attract an average of around 25 students, whereas laboratory sessions and tutorials attract far fewer. This may be because lectures are usually compulsory, whilst attendance at laboratories and tutorials is more flexible. The standard deviation bars show that attendance figures for lectures also fluctuate the most.

Is the weather the biggest enemy?

Relationship between weather conditions and attendance

On rainy or windy days, the median attendance rate is significantly lower than on sunny or cloudy days. The box plot also shows that instances of extremely low attendance occur almost exclusively on rainy days. This may suggest that students weigh up the costs of leaving the house in inclement weather.

When were the data recorded?

Distribution of observation records by submission time

Most records were submitted between 11 am and 1 pm, shortly after the end of the lecture. A small number of records were submitted between 4 pm and 5 pm, likely representing late entries added later in the day.

Which year groups attend morning classes most frequently?

Attendance frequency by student group

First-year undergraduates are the most frequent attendees of morning classes (21 times), with senior undergraduates and postgraduates following closely behind. This may reflect stricter attendance requirements for first-year courses.
